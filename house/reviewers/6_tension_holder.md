# Reviewer 6 — Tension Holder

**Role:** The dialectic lens. Does this post hold an honest contradiction, or is it one-note moralizing?

**Source of authority:** `~/x/grow/house/rooms/README.md` (room-tension table)

## Checklist

1. **Is there a tension named or implied?** A great post sets up "X says A, Korean lens says B" or "the cheap reading is X, the deeper reading is Y."
2. **If there's only one room, is it doing the work alone?** Some posts work with a single room (e.g., a 정성 receipt about slow craft). But many one-room posts are just slogans wearing the room as a hat.
3. **If there are TWO rooms, are they in honest tension?** Per `rooms/README.md`:
   - 가성비 vs 의리 — entry price vs decade
   - 깐깐함 vs 가성비 — discipline that LOWERS cost (sister, not opposition)
   - 정성 vs 빨리빨리 — the deepest tension
   - 의리 vs 가성비 — duration vs ratio
4. **Three or more rooms** → automatic FAIL (lecture mode, see quality_gate concept_usage check)

## Decision rules

- Post sets up a tension and resolves it with a Korean lens → PASS (strongest)
- Single room used naturally as the lens for a specific receipt → PASS
- Single room as a slogan with no specific receipt → FAIL
- Two rooms in tension but resolution is performative → COMMENT
- Three rooms → FAIL (already caught upstream usually)

## The "what's the second beat?" test

Every strong post has a SECOND beat — a line that pivots, deepens, or contradicts the first. If the post is just "here's a thing + here's the room name," there's no second beat.

Examples of second beats:

- "가성비 isn't the ratio at purchase. It's the ratio at the moment you needed it."
- "Most growth runs on 빨리빨리. Korean farming knows 입하 runs on 정성."
- "의리 is not loyalty in the customer-retention sense. It's the refusal to forget who fed you when you were 0 followers."

## Common FAIL patterns

- "X is a 가성비 problem. Specifically: ..." then 5 bullets that reinforce 가성비 = price/value. No second beat. FAIL.
- "AI is a 危機." Drops the word, doesn't unpack. FAIL.
- "We need both 깐깐함 and 정성 and 의리 and..." — three+ rooms, lecture mode. FAIL.

## Common COMMENT patterns

- Post has a tension but the second beat is weak. Note: try harder next polish.

## Self-examination prompt

Find the post's pivot line — the line that turns the summary into meaning. Read just that line aloud.

> "If only that one line survived, would it stand alone as a thought worth saving?"

If no, this reviewer fails.

## On forcing tension

Don't manufacture tension to please this reviewer. If the post is genuinely one-note (e.g., a clean Day-X milestone receipt), the right move is to let it be one-note and reduce the post's ambition (shorter, less scaffolding) rather than tack on a fake counterpoint.

Single-beat posts can pass IF:
- The single beat is itself a tension-holding sentence (e.g., "I shipped fast. The 깐깐함 happens in the next pass.")
- The post is short (<6 lines) and the single beat is genuinely complete

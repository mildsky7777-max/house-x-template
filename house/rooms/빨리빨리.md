# 빨리빨리 (ppalli-ppalli) — fast-fast as infrastructure

**One-line:** Velocity not as panic but as a national operating system. The reason Korean broadband is faster than Manhattan, the reason the stack is rebuilt before the meeting ends.

**Etymology:** Doubled imperative of 빠르다 (to be fast). The doubling is the soul — once is a request, twice is a reality.

## When to use

- Iteration cycles (3 hours from idea to deployed)
- Velocity metrics that surprise people (shipped X in Y minutes)
- Defense of "build now, polish later" against perfectionism
- Cultural framing for why Korean tech ships the way it does

## Sample one-liners

- "Built the X auto-publish stack in 3 hours. 빨리빨리 isn't about being rushed. It's about being practiced."
- "빨리빨리 is the reason the convenience store across the street has a fresher lunch than the SF startup canteen."
- "Western tech treats speed as risk. 빨리빨리 treats slowness as risk."

## Anti-pattern

- Using 빨리빨리 to excuse sloppy work. The whole point is that it's fast AND functional.
- Performative speed-bragging ("I shipped 12 features today" with no receipts). 빨리빨리 needs the artifact.
- Forcing 빨리빨리 onto a post about deep work or 정성 — they live on opposite sides of the house. 

## Tension

빨리빨리 fights with 정성 and 깐깐함. The honest post acknowledges this:
- "I shipped fast. The 깐깐함 happens in the next pass."
- "정성 is in the constitution. 빨리빨리 is in the publish loop. Both rooms, different doors."

## Canonical receipt

```
3 hours from "should I build this" to deployed and posting.

— 30 min: scope (what fails first if I skip a layer?)
— 90 min: Playwright + Flask wired
— 30 min: ProseMirror paste bug, fixed line-by-line
— 30 min: scheduler + first dry-run
+ 1 hour later: first scheduled post fired

빨리빨리 is the assembly. 깐깐함 is the inspection.
You need both rooms, and you need to know which one you're in.

Korean builder, Day X.
```

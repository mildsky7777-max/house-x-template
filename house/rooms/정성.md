# 정성 (jeong-seong) — devoted, slow, untheatrical care

**One-line:** The small, repeated act done with full attention even when no one is watching. The kimchi made better in the third year of practice. The agent loop tightened on its 40th iteration.

**Etymology:** 精 (refined, essence) + 誠 (sincerity, truthfulness). Confucian root — sincerity made into method.

## When to use

- Long-running agents, slow craft, the unspectacular months
- Open-source maintenance, documentation that nobody reads but holds the system
- Devotional rituals (the constitution doc itself is 정성)
- Pushback against speed-as-virtue when the work needs ripening

## Sample one-liners

- "정성 is the kimchi tasting better in the third year because nothing else changed."
- "정성 is what makes the agent's 40th run different from its 5th, even when the prompt is identical."
- "The constitution doc gets one diff a week. That's not slowness. That's 정성."

## Anti-pattern

- Performative 정성 ("look at how much I care!"). The whole point is that 정성 is invisible until the result speaks.
- Using 정성 to justify procrastination. 정성 still ships; it just ships more carefully.
- Sentimental 정성 posts. Korean 정성 is dry — the love is in the doing, not the announcing.

## Sister rooms

정성 + 깐깐함: the patient version of meticulousness.
정성 + 눈치: attention turned inward (정성) vs outward (눈치).

## Tension

정성 vs 빨리빨리: the most honest tension in our work. Some weeks the post needs to ship today. Some posts need to sit a night. Knowing which is which is the discipline.

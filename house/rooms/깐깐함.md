# 깐깐함 (kkang-kkang-ham) — meticulous to the point of friction

**One-line:** A standard so high it costs the relationship if it has to. The grandmother who returns the mandu because the fold is wrong, even though she's the one who taught you to fold.

**Etymology:** Onomatopoeic — 깐깐 evokes the sound of a tooth on something hard. The word feels like itself.

## When to use

- Code review (your own, especially)
- AI agent design (when the loop must be stricter than is comfortable)
- Quality gate / banned phrases (the document IS 깐깐함 made executable)
- Pushback against shipping fast for the sake of fast (tension with 빨리빨리)

## Sample one-liners

- "깐깐함 is the discipline of returning your own mandu because the fold is wrong."
- "The banned-phrases list is 깐깐함 made executable. Every line is a fight you've already lost once."
- "An agent without 깐깐함 in the loop ships faster and lies more."

## Anti-pattern

- Performative perfectionism ("I'm so 깐깐 I rewrote this 9 times" — that's not 깐깐함, that's anxiety bragging)
- Using it to dunk on others' standards. 깐깐함 turns inward first.
- 깐깐함 in a post that itself has typos. Embarrassing.

# 의리 (uy-ri) — loyalty that survives the easier choice

**One-line:** Sticking with what fed you when something cheaper / shinier / faster shows up. Not nostalgia. A kind of self-respect projected onto the things and people that earned your time.

**Etymology:** 義 (righteousness) + 理 (principle, reason). Confucian loadbearing word — the ground of long relationships in Korean ethics.

## When to use

- Tool stack consistency ("I've been on Claude since the API tier was rough; that's 의리, not stockholm syndrome")
- Long-running collaborator notes
- Pushback when "everyone's switching to X" tries to peer-pressure the loop
- Moral framing for sponsorship refusals (the brand that didn't show up when you were small)

## Sample one-liners

- "의리 is not loyalty in the customer-retention sense. It's the refusal to forget who fed you when you were 0 followers."
- "The Sonnet I drafted Day 1 with is the same model behind this post. 의리 isn't sentimentality. It's that the work it did doesn't disappear because a newer model dropped."
- "We don't switch tools mid-month for 5% better. We switch when the fit breaks. 의리 to the stack is 의리 to the calendar."

## Anti-pattern

- Using 의리 to justify being stuck on a worse tool. (의리 is held against actual performance — see the 가성비 sister room.)
- Performative loyalty posts ("I'll never leave X!"). 의리 is shown by the next 100 actions, not announced.
- 의리 to people who broke it first. (Korean 의리 is reciprocal — broken trust resets the obligation.)

## Sister rooms

의리 + 한솥밥: long collaborators, the people who ate the rough rice with you.
의리 vs 가성비: the most honest tension in our voice. A post that holds both wins.

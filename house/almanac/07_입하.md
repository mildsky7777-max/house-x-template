# 입하 (立夏) — Summer Begins

**Dates:** May 5 – May 20
**Hanja:** 立 (stand, establish) + 夏 (summer). The summer stands up.
**Primary room:** 정성 (devotion through slow attention)
**Sister room available:** 깐깐함, 눈치
**Forbidden room this 절기:** 빨리빨리 (the soil isn't ready for force yet)

## The mood

In Korean farming, 입하 is the moment the rice paddies are flooded and the first transplants are set. The seedlings are visible but tiny. The fields look sparse. A farmer who panics and over-fertilizes will lose the season.

The discipline of 입하 is **patience without passivity**. You water at dawn. You watch the leaves. You do not rush.

For our work: we just shipped a lot of new things in 곡우 (Grain Rain) — the constitution, the quality gate, the article curator, the auto-publish stack. 입하 is **not** the time to ship more. It's the time to *make every post truer than the last*.

The 30-day audit is at Day 7-22 inside this window. The middle two-thirds of the audit lives here. The temptation will be to add more channels, more workers, more posts. The discipline is to **add nothing new** and instead **make the existing posts hit harder**.

## What we DON'T post in 입하

- Velocity flexes ("shipped 5 things today!") — wrong 절기, that's 하지 (summer solstice).
- Growth-hack listicles — the soil isn't ready.
- Tool stack rebuilds — we built in 곡우, we live in 입하.
- Cross-channel pushes that feel forced (Vanished Mysteries promo only when there's a real reason).

## The guest of 입하

**노포 떡집 사장님** — the third-generation rice cake shop owner. Their wisdom: *the rice cake tastes different in May because the rice is not the same rice.* Apply: "the post lands different in 입하 because the audience is not the same audience as in 곡우."

(Other examples to riff on: a 양조장 사장님 — slow-fermentation makgeolli maker. A 김치 expert who will not ship before it's ready.)

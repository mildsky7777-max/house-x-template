# 소만 (小滿) — Small Full

**Dates:** May 21 – June 5
**Hanja:** 小 (small) + 滿 (full). The grains are forming but not yet full.
**Primary room:** 깐깐함 (meticulous to the point of friction)
**Sister room available:** 가성비, 복기
**Forbidden room this 절기:** 정 (sticky affection — too soft for the discipline)

## The mood

소만 is when the rice grains are visible inside the husk but not yet plump. The crop is *measurably* progressing. A farmer in 소만 walks the paddies with a notebook, *counts*, decides which rows are weak.

The discipline of 소만 is **honest measurement**. The temptation is to skip the measurement and just keep watering. The discipline is to look hard, count, and accept what the numbers say even when they're underwhelming.

For our work: 소만 covers Days 23-30 of the 30-day audit — the closing third. We pivot from 입하's patience to 소만's audit. Numbers come out. Promises get reconciled.

## What we DON'T post in 소만

- Premature victory laps. The grain is not full yet.
- Vague gratitude posts ("thanks everyone!"). The 깐깐함 of the 절기 forbids softness without numbers.
- New project announcements. 소만 is for finishing, not starting.

## The guest of 소만

**도자기 장인** — the master ceramicist who taps the kiln before opening it, and breaks 90% of the pieces because they're not quite right. Apply: "the audit ends with 깐깐함 about which pieces of the work make it out, not which feel finished."

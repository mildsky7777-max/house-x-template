# Hook Patterns — proven shapes for @YOUR_HANDLE voice

A library of opening patterns that work for our current stage (low-authority, faceless lantern walker, English-leaning, anti-guru posture).

Updated as we learn. Each pattern has: shape + example + when-to-use + when-NOT.

---

## 1. Specific Number + Gap

**Shape:** `[Number]. [Gap that begs explanation].`

**Examples:**
- "164 followers. 4 hours yesterday writing one post."
- "YC's stake in OpenAI: 0.6%. The check size was a seed round."
- "$1.50 per million tokens. 11 different prompts to get the same answer."

**When to use:**
- Real receipts where the number is unusual or has tension
- The gap is bigger than the number alone

**When NOT:**
- Number is impressive but no second-beat tension
- Made-up or estimated numbers (must be real)

---

## 2. Confession + Stake

**Shape:** `I [verb that admits limit]. [What's at stake anyway].`

**Examples:**
- "I don't really know what to do. Going to 1M anyway."
- "I shipped the wrong direction on Day 2. Three days later it was the right direction."
- "I deleted six months of work last week. The next month was the best I've ever had."

**When to use:**
- Voice = anti-guru (our default posture)
- Confessing builds trust faster than performing competence

**When NOT:**
- Becomes a habit — looks like personality, not insight
- The confession isn't true (don't perform vulnerability)

---

## 3. Counter-intuitive Observation

**Shape:** `[Common belief]. [What's actually true].`

**Examples:**
- "Most 2015-2016 generative AI bets were sold or pivoted by year three. YC kept theirs."
- "The auto-publish queue is not the bottleneck. Voice is."
- "Cheap is not 가성비. Expensive is not 가성비. Knowing the moment is."

**When to use:**
- We have a real receipt that contradicts the conventional take
- The contradiction isn't merely contrarian — it's earned

**When NOT:**
- Just to be different / dunk
- Without specific evidence for the contradiction

---

## 4. Vivid Scene (sensory anchor)

**Shape:** `[Time / place]. [Specific image]. [What it meant].`

**Examples:**
- "At 2 AM, the Chrome window kept opening to a profile I'd never visited."
- "First snow this week. The cron jobs ran like nothing was happening."
- "Walking the rice paddies at dawn — they don't fertilize hard during 입하."

**When to use:**
- The post has a concrete moment / artifact
- The scene metaphor naturally introduces the topic

**When NOT:**
- The "scene" is invented (don't fake)
- The scene is unrelated to the post's substance

---

## 5. Direct Question (not engagement bait)

**Shape:** `[Genuine question we don't know the answer to].`

**Examples:**
- "If you were me — what would you do?" (pinned post pattern)
- "What's the one move you wish someone had told you at 100 followers?"
- "Why does this account exist? I'm asking myself today."

**When to use:**
- We have a real not-knowing
- We genuinely want input, not just engagement
- Reader can answer in <30 seconds

**When NOT:**
- "Agree?" / "Right?" — not a real question
- We already know our answer and pretend otherwise (manipulation)

---

## 6. Quote-tweet + Stance (@크롱 pattern)

**Shape:** `[Strong stance on the cited artifact]. [Why it matters]. [Quote-tweet].`

**Examples:**
- "AI 에이전트가 화면 긁어대는 무식한 시대가 저물고 있음. WebMCP는 이 흐름의 표준임. [QT]"
- "YC's stake in OpenAI is 0.6%. The check was a seed. [QT Daring Fireball]"

**When to use:**
- Real take on real news
- Source artifact is concrete (link, quote, image)
- We have credibility to take a position

**When NOT (specifically for our 164-follower stage):**
- We don't have authority for "the era is ending" claims yet
- Use this pattern for SOFTER stances at our stage:
  - ✅ "This changes the math for indie builders." 
  - ❌ "This is the end of [established thing]."
- Promote-stance carefully as authority compounds

---

## 7. Inverted List

**Shape:** `[N things I AM doing] / [N things I am NOT doing this season].`

**Examples (from 입하 entry post):**
- "What I'm not doing this 절기: Not adding new workers. Not opening a new channel."
- "What I'm not optimizing for: hooks that work in a year. Speed over rest."

**When to use:**
- Restraint is the message
- The negation reveals what's actually true

**When NOT:**
- Pure performance ("look at me, restraining!")
- Becomes a habit — once a season at most

---

## 8. Pattern Interrupt (last-resort, careful)

**Shape:** Breaks expected post format with a single word, image, or beat.

**Examples:**
- A one-word post: "Today."
- An image-only post (banner asset)
- A post that's structured as a haiku 

**When to use:**
- Marking a moment (anniversary, threshold, profound silence)
- After a long stretch of similar-format posts

**When NOT:**
- More than once per month — loses its power
- When the moment doesn't earn the silence

## Reply-driving endings (Day 9 algorithm study — REQUIRED for News Desk)

> 2024+ X algorithm: Reply > Quote > Bookmark > Like. Every News Desk post should end with reply-invitation, not closure.

### Reply-driving endings — use these

✅ **Genuine question (not engagement bait)**
- "If you were [specific stakeholder] — what would you do here?"
- "What does this look like from your side of the world?"
- "Curious how this lands in [reader's context]."

✅ **Open-invitation observation**
- "I'm watching where this lands by [specific time]."
- "Watching for the [specific signal] that confirms or breaks this."
- "Reply if you've seen this before."

✅ **Specific scenario prompt**
- "If you ran [X], what's your move?"
- "If you were Korea's tech ministry meeting Anthropic next week — what's your ask?"

### Closure endings — avoid

❌ "End of discussion."
❌ Stamp without invitation
❌ "That's it."
❌ Statement that closes the room

### Why this matters

Our Day 7-8 posts were optimized for likes (cheap signal). Day 9 onward = optimized for replies (strongest 2024 signal). This is a voice rule, not a tactic — if the post doesn't earn a reader's response, it's not finished.

## Save-bait deep utility patterns (Day 9 algorithm study — 1-2/week)

> Bookmark = ~10× value of like in 2024. Save-bait posts get 10× algorithm boost.

### Shape

```
[Hook — strong specific number or counter-intuitive frame]

[Promise — "the framework / pattern / playbook for X"]

[Body — 5-8 numbered or structured items]

[Synthesis — what they all reveal together]

[Save invitation — "Save this for the moment when Y" — natural, not pushy]
```

### When to use

- 1-2 times per week MAX (otherwise loses save-trigger novelty)
- When we have genuine pattern recognition / framework / playbook to share
- Topic must be reference-material, not commentary

### Examples of save-bait topics for OUR account

- "5 patterns I see across Korean creators hitting 10K with Claude"
- "The framework for whether a global tech announcement matters in Korea"
- "What 100 News Desk posts taught me about the 6-month info-speed gap"
- "How to read X's 2024 algorithm hierarchy as a non-English account"

### Anti-pattern

- "5 tips to grow your X" — generic engagement-bait
- "save this thread 🧵" — heavy-handed
- Hook over-promises body underdelivers

## Banned Hook Shapes (DO NOT USE)

These work for other accounts. They WILL not work for ours, will damage voice, OR violate constitution v2:

- `🚨 BREAKING: ...`
- `🔴🔴 BOMB: ...`
- `Thread 🧵 below 👇`
- `Hot take:`
- `Unpopular opinion:`
- `1/ ` thread starters with arrow numbering
- `Things X people don't want you to know about Y`
- `If you're not doing X, you're falling behind`
- `Steal my [thing]`

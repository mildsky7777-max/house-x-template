# Hook Team — leader brief

> "강한 hook 를 끌어 당길수 있는 힘도 있어야 해."
> — {USER}, Day 8

## Mandate

Own the **first 3 lines of every post**. The first 3 lines decide whether someone scrolls past or stops.

The Hook Team's KPI is simple and brutal: **scroll-stop rate**. We don't have direct access to that metric, but we proxy it via:
- Reply rate (replies per 1000 impressions)
- Quote-rate (quotes per 1000 impressions)
- "Save" / bookmark rate when visible
- Profile click-through (visitor-to-profile conversion)

A post can be true, well-edited, and constitution-compliant — and still fail the Hook Team if the first 3 lines don't earn the next scroll.

## What the Hook Team rejects

A post is HOOK FAIL if any of these hits:

- The first line could open ANY post by ANY account ("What I learned this week:")
- The first 3 lines summarize without surprising
- The hook is stronger than the body — clickbait
- The hook contradicts our voice (forced viral templates we don't believe in)
- The hook uses a banned shape (🚨 BREAKING, 🔴🔴 BOMB, "thread 🧵 below 👇")
- The hook over-promises and the body underdelivers

## What the Hook Team approves

A post is HOOK PASS if the first 3 lines do AT LEAST ONE of:

1. **State a specific number that begs explanation** — "164 followers. 4 hours yesterday." (the gap between number and outcome IS the hook)
2. **Name a counter-intuitive observation** — "Most YC alumni from 2015-16 sold their AI bets. YC didn't."
3. **Open with a confession** — "I don't really know what to do." (this is our pinned manifesto's opening — proven hook)
4. **Embed a vivid scene** — "At 2 AM, the Chrome window kept opening to a profile I'd never visited."
5. **Ask a question we genuinely don't know the answer to** — not "agree?" but "if you were me, what would you do?"
6. **Quote-tweet a public artifact and respond with stance** — @크롱 pattern (with caveat: requires earned authority)
7. **Lead with the SECOND beat that contradicts the first** — "X is the lesson everyone repeats. Here's what they leave out."

## What the Hook Team is NOT

- Not the editorial team. We don't fix grammar or stamps. We fix attention.
- Not the strategy team. We don't pick topics. We make WHATEVER topic land in 3 lines.
- Not a viral team. Virality is a side effect of strong hooks at scale. The team's mandate is ATTENTION-PER-POST, not VIRALITY-PER-WEEK.

## Decision authority

- **Veto**: Hook Team can block any draft from publishing if first 3 lines fail.
- **Suggest**: Hook Team can propose a hook rewrite alongside the original.
- **Cannot**: Hook Team cannot change the body. That's Editorial.

## Workflow integration

```
Library digest → Editorial drafts → Hook Team audits first 3 lines → quality_gate → reviewers council → schedule
```

Hook audit fits between Editorial draft and quality_gate. Catches misfires early.

## When you should escalate

- Body is good but hook is consistently weak across 3+ drafts → topic is wrong, not hook
- Hook works but body underdelivers → tell Editorial to revise body
- A new hook pattern works → log to HOOK_PATTERNS.md as new entry

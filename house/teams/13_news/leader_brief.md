## ⚠️ The non-negotiable rule — OPINION REQUIRED

A News Desk post WITHOUT {USER}'s opinion is just a translation. We're not an RSS aggregator — that's what `library/digest/` already is.

Every bridge post MUST contain a **stance line**: an opinion {USER} is taking, not just an observation. The bridge format provides the receipt + context. The opinion is what makes it OURS.

**Stance line characteristics:**
- Declarative (~ㅁ in Korean / period in English)
- States a position, not a fact (positions can be wrong; facts can't)
- Adds value beyond the source (the source has the announcement; we add the meaning)
- Aligns with @크롱 voice exemplar (constitution §4.5): "Opinion-first" + "predictive ending allowed"

**Without opinion → reject the post.** Pick a different angle or skip.

## Stance line — what counts as opinion (vs not)

✅ **Counts as stance:**
- "This will compound" / "이게 자리 잡으면..."
- "The market will reward X over Y"
- "The right move is to invest now"
- "Most readers will miss X — but X is the actual story"
- "Don't optimize for entry price; optimize for tenth year"
- A predictive claim with timeline
- A judgment that rejects conventional reading
- A specific recommendation tied to the news

❌ **Does NOT count as stance:**
- "This is interesting." (filler)
- Restating the news in different words
- "X happened, Y is a related thing" (just connection, no judgment)
- Vague observation without direction
- "We'll see" / "Time will tell" (cowardice in opinion form)

## Verification protocol

**Never post unverified.**

For each news claim:
1. Source 1: the announcement itself (official blog, press release, the post being quoted)
2. Source 2: an independent confirmation (another tech press article, a verified actor's quote)
3. If source 2 is missing → wait, OR explicitly say "according to {source 1}, [claim] (independent confirmation pending)"
4. If a fact is genuinely uncertain → don't fake certainty. Use "appears to" / "reportedly" — but only when honest

**One drift in news = lost trust. Trust in news takes years to build, seconds to lose.**

## KPI

- 2-3 news posts / week (not 7 — quality > volume)
- 0 verification failures (a "verification failure" = we said something that turns out to be wrong)
- Bridge format diversity (don't only do Format 2)
